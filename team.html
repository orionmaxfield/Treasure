Our therapists

We're a small practice on purpose. You'll get to know your therapist, and they'll get to know you. Each of us has a different background and style; all of us are committed to careful, current, respectful care.

Joe Arzola, LCSW

Psychotherapist and practice administrator

Joe loves getting to know people and having meaningful conversations with them. A veteran who served for 12 years, he has worked in mental health for more than 21 years with people from many backgrounds and with many different needs.

His philosophy is that you should take the lead in your own treatment, because that's what leads to lasting change. Investing in counseling is investing in yourself: just like our bodies need attention, so do our minds and emotions.

Works with

Trauma, addictions, eating disorders, anxiety, depression, couples counseling, and developmental delays.

Jennifer Arzola, LPC

Psychotherapist

Jennifer's work is grounded in the belief that we're wired for connection, and that our deepest sense of safety and belonging comes from our relationships. When those relationships have felt inconsistent or painful, we can end up living in patterns of survival rather than security.

In sessions, she works through an attachment lens, slowing down to notice the emotions underneath the surface and helping you understand and express them in new ways. She sees therapy as a collaborative relationship where you don't have to figure things out alone. If your faith is important to you, she'll thoughtfully include it; if not, your autonomy is always respected.

Works with

Adults 18 and older. Anxiety, depression, trauma, grief and loss, life transitions, and identity concerns.

Charlene Holloway, LMSW

Psychotherapist

Charlene believes every person has an inherent capacity for healing that can be nurtured through empathetic guidance and personalized support. She creates a safe, non-judgmental space where trust can be built and you can share your concerns, wants, and goals at your own pace.

With more than 16 years of experience helping children, teens, adults, and couples in crisis, she draws on Cognitive Behavioral Therapy, Dialectical Behavior Therapy, Polyvagal Theory, EMDR, and sand, art, and play therapy.

Works with

Children, teens, adults, and couples. PTSD and complex trauma, depression, anxiety, ODD, and relationship issues. Trained in EMDR and play therapy.
