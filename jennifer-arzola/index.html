About Jennifer

Jennifer's work is grounded in a simple belief: we're wired for connection, and our deepest sense of safety and belonging comes from our relationships.

In sessions she works through an attachment lens and draws on emotionally focused therapy, slowing down to notice the feelings underneath the surface and helping you understand and express them in new ways. Therapy with Jennifer is collaborative. You don't have to figure things out alone. If your faith matters to you, she'll thoughtfully include it; if not, your autonomy is always respected.

Common questions

How do I book an appointment with Jennifer?

Use the appointment request form with Jennifer pre-selected, or call 208-479-1116. We reply within one business day. Jennifer also offers a free 15-minute phone consultation so you can ask questions first.

Does Jennifer take insurance?

Yes. Treasure Valley Mental Health accepts most major plans, including Idaho Medicaid, with TriCare as the one exception. Tell us your insurer when you reach out and we'll verify coverage. See fees and insurance.

Can I see Jennifer online?

Yes. Jennifer meets clients in person at our office at 6126 W. State Street in Boise, and by telehealth anywhere in Idaho.
