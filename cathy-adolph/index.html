About Cathy

Cathy is a dual-discipline wellness professional pursuing her Master of Social Work at Boise State University while providing Community-Based Rehabilitation Services (CBRS) for Treasure Valley Mental Health. A Registered Dietitian and Certified Personal Trainer since 2003, she bridges the gap between physical and mental health.

Cathy also brings a decade of crisis intervention experience as a team lead for the Idaho Crisis and Suicide Hotline, where she champions client autonomy and goal-oriented care. In her free time, she stays active as a runner, triathlete, rock climber, and drummer.

Cathy's work is part of our Community-Based Rehabilitation Services: practical, one-on-one support in your home and community for the skills that make the rest of the week go better.

Common questions

How do I start CBRS with Cathy?

Use the appointment request form with Cathy pre-selected, or call 208-479-1116 and mention CBRS. We'll set up an assessment and walk you through eligibility.

Do I need Medicaid?

Yes. CBRS is an Idaho Medicaid benefit. If you're not sure whether you qualify, ask and we'll help you find out. Therapy with our other clinicians is available with most insurance or on a sliding fee scale.

Does Cathy provide therapy?

Cathy provides CBRS, which is skills-based support in your community, and works alongside your therapist here. She is a Master of Social Work student supervised by our licensed clinicians.
