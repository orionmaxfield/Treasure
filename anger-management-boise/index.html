Anger management in Boise

Anger isn't the problem. What it's costing you is. Whether you're here on your own or because a court, an employer, or someone you love asked you to be, you'll get practical tools and a straight-talking therapist who's been doing this for more than 21 years.

Request an appointment Call 208-479-1116 for a free 15-minute consultation

Does this sound familiar?

  • You go from calm to furious faster than you can catch it
  • Arguments at home keep escalating, and you regret what you said
  • Anger has cost you a relationship, a job, or a legal charge
  • You've been told to complete an anger management program
  • You feel tense, irritable, or resentful most of the time
  • You want to be a calmer partner or parent and don't know how

You don't need to check every box, and you don't need a diagnosis to reach out.

The Wednesday evening group

Our anger management group meets every Wednesday from 6pm to 8pm at our office on West State Street in Boise, led by Joe Arzola, LCSW. You'll learn to recognize your triggers, catch anger earlier, calm your body down, and respond instead of react. The group satisfies most court-ordered anger management requirements, and we provide documentation of attendance and completion.

Individual anger counseling

If a group isn't the right fit, or you want to go deeper, individual sessions look at what's underneath the anger: stress, trauma, shame, feeling disrespected or powerless. We use CBT, DBT skills, and trauma-focused work when past experiences are driving the reaction.

What to expect

No lectures. You'll get concrete strategies you can use the same week, and a place to be honest about what's actually going on. Understanding your own pattern is usually the turning point.

Common questions

Does the group satisfy a court order?

It satisfies most court-ordered anger management requirements. Bring your paperwork to your first session so we can confirm it meets what your court or probation officer requires, and we'll document your attendance and completion.

How do I join the group?

Call 208-479-1116 or use the request form and mention the anger management group. We'll do a brief intake and tell you when to start.

How much does it cost?

Group sessions are lower cost than individual therapy. Many insurance plans cover group therapy, and we have a sliding fee scale. Ask when you call. See fees and insurance.

Can I do anger management online?

Individual anger counseling is available by telehealth anywhere in Idaho. The group meets in person.

Getting started

  1. Reach out. Use the form or call. We reply within one business day.
  2. Free 15-minute call. Ask questions, tell us what's going on, and we'll match you with a therapist.
  3. First session. In person on West State Street or by telehealth anywhere in Idaho.
