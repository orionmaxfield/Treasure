Privacy

Plain-language notes on what this website collects and what it doesn't.

The contact form

When you send us a request through the website, you share your name, phone number, email, a preferred time to be contacted, and anything you choose to write in the optional message box. That information is delivered to our office email so we can get back to you. It's used only to respond to your request and is not sold or shared with advertisers.

The form is processed by Formspree, a third-party service. Because email is not a secure medium, please keep the message box to scheduling details and share anything sensitive with your therapist directly.

What we don't do

  • We don't use advertising trackers or sell any information.
  • We don't require an account or a login to use this site.

Third-party services

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts and an embedded map from Google Maps. Those services may log basic technical information, such as your IP address, according to Google's own privacy policy.

Your clinical records

Your therapy records are protected under HIPAA and handled separately from this website. Your therapist can explain how your records are kept and your rights regarding them.
