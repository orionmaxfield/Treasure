If you need help right now

This form isn't monitored around the clock, and we're not an emergency service. If you or someone you're with is in danger or in crisis:

  • Call or text 988, the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline. Free, confidential, 24 hours a day.
  • Call 911 for a medical emergency.
  • Dial 211 for the Idaho CareLine to find local support and resources.

Reaching out in a hard moment is a brave thing to do. Please don't wait.
