What we help with

You don't need a diagnosis or the right words to get started. If something here sounds like you, or even if it doesn't, reach out and we'll help you figure out the right next step.

Wondering who you'd work with? Meet our therapists and see what each of them focuses on. Every one of them offers a free 15-minute phone consultation, so you can ask questions before you book.

Individual therapy

Private, one-on-one sessions for adults, teens, and children. Our therapists draw on approaches including Cognitive Behavioral Therapy, Dialectical Behavior Therapy, EMDR, attachment-focused work, and play therapy, and match the approach to you rather than the other way around.

Couples therapy

For partners who want to communicate better, repair after a rupture, or reconnect. Sessions are a neutral space where both of you get heard.

Family therapy

Support for families working through conflict, parenting, major transitions, or reunification therapy when a parent and child are rebuilding their relationship.

Faith-based counseling

If your faith is part of your identity and healing, we can thoughtfully include it. If not, that's respected too. You set the terms.

Screening and assessments

A structured way to understand what's going on and what kind of care fits, for you or someone you love.

Treatment planning

A road map we build together so you know what we're working toward and how we'll get there.

Anger management group

A weekly group led by Joe Arzola, LCSW, on Wednesdays from 6pm to 8pm at our office. You'll learn practical techniques and strategies to manage anger and the emotions underneath it. The group satisfies most court-ordered anger management requirements. Ask about joining or see the group on Psychology Today.

Care coordination and CBRS

Care coordination links you to community resources like housing, healthcare, and employment services. Community-Based Rehabilitation Services (CBRS) help you build practical skills for daily life in your own community.

Specialties

A closer look at the concerns we see most, who on our team works with each, and what to expect.

Anxiety therapy in Boise

Racing thoughts, a chest that won't unclench, nights spent rehearsing tomorrow. Anxiety is the most common reason people come to us, and one of the most treatable.

Depression therapy in Boise

Depression lies. It says nothing will help and that you should handle it alone. If you've read this far, some part of you disagrees. Start there.

Trauma and PTSD therapy in Boise

Trauma isn't only what happened to you. It's what your mind and body are still doing about it. That part can change, and it often changes faster than people expect.

Couples therapy in Boise

You keep having the same fight. Or you've stopped fighting and started living like roommates. Either way, the distance between you is the problem, not the person across from you.

Child and teen therapy in Boise

Kids don't always have the words for what they're feeling. That's why they show it instead: meltdowns, withdrawal, worry, trouble at school. We help them find the words, and help you help them.

Grief and loss counseling in Boise

Grief doesn't follow a schedule, and it isn't a problem to fix. But you don't have to carry it alone, and you don't have to pretend you're further along than you are.

Anger management in Boise

Anger isn't the problem. What it's costing you is. Whether you're here on your own or because a court, an employer, or someone you love asked you to be, you'll get practical tools and a straight-talking therapist who's been doing this for more than 21 years.

Online therapy anywhere in Idaho

If you live in Twin Falls, McCall, Pocatello, or a town too small to have a therapist, or you're in Boise and can't add another drive to your week, you can still see us. Every therapist here offers telehealth to anyone in Idaho.

Faith-based counseling in Boise

Your faith is part of how you understand yourself, your relationships, and what healing means. You shouldn't have to leave it at the door of a therapist's office, and you shouldn't have to explain it, either.

Community-Based Rehabilitation Services (CBRS) in Boise

Therapy happens in an office for an hour a week. CBRS happens where life happens: at home, at school, in the community, working on the real-world skills that make the rest of the week go better.

ADHD therapy in Boise

ADHD isn't a lack of trying. If anything, you've been trying harder than everyone around you and getting less credit for it. Therapy can't change how your brain is wired, but it can change how much it costs you.

Reunification therapy in Boise

When a parent and child have been apart, whether because of a custody dispute, a difficult separation, or time away, getting back together isn't as simple as scheduling a visit. Reunification therapy makes the process safe, structured, and paced for the child.
