Fees and insurance

Money shouldn't be the reason you don't get help. Here's how it works.

Not sure what you'll pay? Start with a free 15-minute phone consultation. Tell us your insurance and what you're looking for, and we'll tell you exactly what a session will cost before you commit to anything. Request a call or phone 208-479-1116.

Insurance we accept

We're in network with most major plans, including Idaho Medicaid. Plans our therapists currently bill include:

  • Aetna
  • Anthem
  • Blue Cross
  • Blue Shield
  • Cigna and Evernorth
  • First Choice Health
  • Humana
  • Idaho Medicaid
  • Molina
  • UnitedHealthcare
  • and others

The one plan we can't bill is TriCare. Coverage can vary by therapist, so if your plan isn't listed or you want to be sure, tell us your insurer when you reach out and we'll verify it for you. You can also see our current plan list on our Psychology Today profile.

What a session costs

If you're using insurance, you'll pay your plan's usual copay or coinsurance. If you're paying out of pocket, sessions range from $80 to $300 depending on the therapist, the type of session, and its length. As an example, a standard individual session with Jennifer Arzola is $125. We'll confirm your exact rate before your first appointment.

We accept cash, Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, Health Savings Account cards, Venmo, PayPal, Zelle, ACH bank transfer, and wire.

Affordable options

Money shouldn't be the reason you don't get help. If you're paying out of pocket, or your insurance doesn't cover therapy, you still have options:

  • Sliding fee scale. A discounted rate based on income and family size, using the federal poverty guidelines. Bring proof of income and we'll set it up. No one is turned away because they can't pay.
  • Reduced-rate sessions. Lower-cost appointments with select clinicians, including sessions with our supervised counseling intern. These usually have the shortest wait.
  • Flexible payment arrangements. If a single payment is hard, ask us about spreading it out.

To apply or ask questions, mention it on the contact form, email admin@tvmhtherapy.com, or call 208-479-1116.

Good faith estimate

If you're uninsured or choose not to use insurance, you have the right to receive a Good Faith Estimate of expected charges before your first appointment, under the federal No Surprises Act. Just ask.
