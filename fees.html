Fees and insurance

Money shouldn't be the reason you don't get help. Here's how it works.

Insurance

We accept most insurance plans, including Idaho Medicaid. The one exception is TriCare. If you're not sure whether your plan is covered, tell us your insurer when you reach out and we'll check for you.

Affordable options

If you're paying out of pocket, or your insurance doesn't cover therapy, you still have options:

  • Sliding fee scale. A discounted rate based on family size and income. No one is turned away because they can't pay.
  • Reduced-rate sessions. We offer lower-cost appointments with select clinicians. Ask us about availability.

To apply or ask questions, mention it on the contact form, email admin@tvmhtherapy.com, or call 208-479-1116.

Good faith estimate

If you're uninsured or choose not to use insurance, you have the right to receive a Good Faith Estimate of expected charges before your first appointment, under the federal No Surprises Act. Just ask.
