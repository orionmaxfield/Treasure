How we can help

Most people come to us for anxiety or depression. Many stay for the steady, honest support of a therapist who knows them.

Individual therapy

One-on-one sessions for adults, teens, and children. A private place to work through anxiety, depression, trauma, grief, stress, life transitions, and the things that are hard to say out loud anywhere else.

Couples therapy

Help for partners who keep having the same argument, feel more like roommates than a couple, or want to rebuild trust and closeness.

Family therapy

Support for families navigating conflict, parenting challenges, big transitions, or reunification after time apart.

Community support services

Screenings and assessments, treatment planning, care coordination, and Community-Based Rehabilitation Services (CBRS) to connect you with housing, healthcare, employment, and skills for daily life.
